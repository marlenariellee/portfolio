01 / Meet the creator

Creative work with a research background.

My background spans content creation, digital marketing, UX research, entrepreneurship, and community work. I earned my master’s degree in Information, focused on UX Research and Design, at the University of Michigan. That training shapes how I approach a brief: understand the audience, identify the message, and make the next step clear.

I’m comfortable in front of the camera, behind the edit, and in a room full of people. My work ranges from product storytelling and beauty content to student workshops, artist promotion, and alumni interviews.

For brands, that means I can contribute both the content and the thinking around it: what to say, which format fits, and how a piece connects to the wider campaign.

06 / Working together

From the brief to the final content.

  1. Define the project

    Share the brand, audience, campaign goal, deliverables, timing, budget, and where the content will be used.

  2. Agree on the direction

    We align on the concept, message, scope, approvals, and any product or event logistics.

  3. Create and review

    I produce the agreed content, with the review and revision process defined in the project scope.

  4. Deliver or publish

    Final files or scheduled posts follow the agreed formats, deadlines, and usage terms.

Have a project in mind?

Email me with a short brief and the timeline. Include whether you need content for your own channels, a post on mine, or both.
